I'm Awoyele Victor Ayomide, the creator of SONIVOX. I built this because, as a developer experimenting with AI assistants and voice technology, I know how frustrating it can be when useful voice APIs are too expensive or difficult to get started with.

SONIVOX is my way of making speech generation more approachable for developers, students, independent builders, and teams bringing new ideas to life.

THE MISSION

Make it easier to add a voice to the things you build. Experiment freely, learn by building, and turn ideas into working products.
